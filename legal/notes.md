# legal/notes.md — the whole gate

This project is a **fiction inspired by** a real person. Inspired-by fiction is low legal exposure. The entire real-world guardrail is two lines. It lives here, once, not on every page.

## 1. The real person

- Invent freely about the **character**. The protagonist is not the real Chuck Williams and is not presented as him.
- Do **not** present a **defamatory falsehood as fact about the real Chuck Williams** — or any other real, named person. No invented crimes, scandals, or private-life claims attributed to the actual historical person.
- The real Chuck Williams (kitchenware merchant, 1915–2015) is a *touchstone*. Don't confuse him with the unrelated Charles "Chuck" Williams (Sperry executive, Williams Institute at UCLA, d. 2023).

## 1a. Fictional-analogue rule (added at premise lock, 2026-08-06)

Every institution in the story is an invented composite — the Magazine, the Store downtown, the Bank, the Movement (`bible/world/fictionalization.md`). Real institutions (Ebony, Marshall Field's, Seaway, Operation PUSH, etc.) never act in the story and are never depicted as story characters; at most they exist as unremarked background texture. Invented names must be checked against real institutions and living people before locking.

## 2. Real trademarks & brands

- Do **not** reproduce real trademarks — Williams-Sonoma logos, catalog copy, product photography — as if genuine, especially in `deck/`.
- If a real brand is referenced anywhere the audience sees it, log it here with a one-line note on how it's used.

## Rights posture

- Default: **independent fiction**, not authorized, not a biography. No rights are asserted or required for an inspired-by work that invents its protagonist and world.
- Revisit only if the project ever moves toward using the real name/marks directly.

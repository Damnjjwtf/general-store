## Research hygiene note

There is more than one "Chuck Williams." A **different** Charles "Chuck" Williams (a Sperry/RCA executive, founder of the Williams Institute at UCLA, d. 2023) dominates search results and is **not** our touchstone. Ours is the kitchenware merchant, b. 1915, d. 2015. Don't fuse the two. Since our protagonist is invented anyway, this only matters for keeping the *inspiration* material clean.

# research/ — inspiration, not canon

Everything in this folder is **raw material to enrich the fiction**. None of it is binding. The show does not have to honor any fact here; it mines it for texture.

- This is not a source-verification system. There are no claim tags, no tiers, no ledger.
- Real people and brands appearing here are inspiration. What the *show* asserts lives in `bible/`, and is invented.
- When you pull a detail from here into the world, it stops being "what really happened" and becomes "ours." Record it in `bible/`, not here.

The one guardrail on real people and brands lives in `legal/notes.md`. It is short.

## Contents

- `chuck-williams.md` — the primary touchstone: the real merchant and the specialty-retail revolution, as ore.
- `sources.md` — an informal list of where the touchstone material came from. Provenance for our own use, nothing more.

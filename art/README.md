# art/

Visual development for the show **and** the pitch. The plan and tooling live in `docs/art-stack.md`; this folder holds the actual output.

## What's here
- `tone-board.md` — the premise-agnostic visual thesis (palette, light, texture, framing, anti-look). **Started.**
- `deck-system.md` — the pitch's design system (next).
- `title/` — original *General Store* title-treatment exploration (next).
- `moodboards/` — curated reference sets; images in `../assets/`, linked here and into the Obsidian graph.

## Two rules for everything in here
1. **Inspiration vs. adopted design.** A reference or AI moodboard is inspiration (note it, keep it loose). The moment a look is *adopted*, record it as canon on the matching `bible/` entity — the store's look on the `Place`, a character's wardrobe on the `Character`. See `bible/graph.md`.
2. **Legal.** Original title art only; no real Williams-Sonoma marks/catalog/product shots as genuine; no AI real-person likeness. See `legal/notes.md`.
